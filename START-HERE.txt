==========================================================
 STORYFORGE AI — START HERE (beginner guide)
==========================================================

WHAT THIS IS
A free app that turns your story ideas into narrated videos
(MP4 files you can upload to YouTube). No subscription, no
credit card, no paid AI service.

----------------------------------------------------------
 OPTION A — JUST USE THE LINK (easiest, nothing to install)
----------------------------------------------------------
Your current live link is saved in PUBLIC-LINK.txt.
Open it on any phone, tablet, or computer browser (Chrome,
Safari, Edge). On Android Chrome use Menu > "Install app";
on iPhone Safari use Share > "Add to Home Screen" to get an
icon like a normal app.

Note: this free link is temporary. If it ever stops loading,
ask your assistant to create a new one, or use Option B.

----------------------------------------------------------
 OPTION B — RUN IT ON YOUR OWN COMPUTER (permanent, free)
----------------------------------------------------------
1. Install these two free programs (one-time):
     Python:   https://www.python.org/downloads/  (tick "Add
               python.exe to PATH" during install on Windows)
     Node.js:  https://nodejs.org/  (pick the LTS button)
2. Unzip this folder anywhere, e.g. your Desktop.
3. Windows: double-click  start-all.bat
   Mac/Linux: open Terminal in this folder and run:
              ./start-all.sh
4. Wait for it to finish, then open this in Chrome:
              http://localhost:8000
5. To make it a clickable app icon: Chrome menu >
   "Cast, save and share" > "Install page as app".

Your videos, scripts and voices stay on your own computer.

----------------------------------------------------------
 NEED HELP?
----------------------------------------------------------
- Full walkthrough (phone access, auto-start, free hosting):
  see ACCESS-GUIDE.md and DEPLOY.md
- If a voice is missing: run backend/download-voices.bat
- Minimum computer: 4 GB RAM is comfortable; the app itself
  runs on 2 GB. Video-generation models (Mode A) need a
  gaming NVIDIA GPU; the standard story-to-video pipeline
  works on ordinary computers.
